Speaker echo probe (V0, manual)

Measures how much of a synthetic Kokoro voice played from this page leaks into the microphone with Chrome's echo cancellation off and on. Play it through your speakers at the volume you really use, sit where you really sit, and stay silent for the whole run (about 2 minutes). No microphone audio leaves this page: nothing is uploaded or saved; the numbers below are the only output. Run it in the same Chrome profile and the same audio device as the HUD.
